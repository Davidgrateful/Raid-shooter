## 1. Cover

**Raid Shooter** — a browser arcade shooter with a real competitive
leaderboard, an on-chain cosmetics economy, and a boss roster that keeps
growing. Built, shipped, and iterated in the open.

- **Platform:** Browser, canvas engine, no download
- **Chain:** Base (wallet login, cosmetics marketplace)
- **Status:** Live, actively shipping weekly

## 2. The gap

Most browser shooters have no reason to come back tomorrow — no rank, no
rival, no stakes. Most Web3 games lead with the wallet and bolt a game on
after, so the game is thin and "play" is really just clicking through a
token loop.

1. **No reason to return.** A high score with nobody to compare it to isn't
   a hook — it's a number that resets when you close the tab.
2. **Pay-to-win kills trust.** The moment spending buys a competitive edge,
   the leaderboard stops meaning anything, and players know it immediately.
3. **Wallet friction, day one.** Forcing a connect before someone even sees
   the game guarantees most people never see the game.

## 3. The product

Anyone opens the link and is shooting within seconds — no wallet required.
A wallet upgrades your identity to verified; it never touches your stats.
That single rule is why the leaderboard is worth competing on.

- **12 pilots, 6 drones.** One free starter; every other pilot is
  purchase-only cosmetics — buy the look, not the win.
- **20 enemies + 6 bosses.** 3 cosmic, 3 alien-biological (Plasma Medusa,
  Hive Queen, Xeno Monarch) on random rotation — no two runs the same.
- **4 competitive boards.** All-time, weekly ladder, sponsored Cup
  tournaments, and a seeded Daily Run.
- **Real game feel.** Hitstop on kills, danger warnings at low HP,
  off-screen threat arrows — the polish pass most browser games skip.
- **Guest-first identity.** A durable device identity means guests keep
  their rank even without a wallet, across iOS Safari's aggressive cookie
  limits.
- **Cross-board integrity.** Moderation (derank/ban) clears a cheater from
  every board at once, with a one-click restore for mistakes.

## 5. Business model

Revenue that never touches the leaderboard.

| Lever | Detail |
|---|---|
| Cosmetics | Pilots, drones, skins, trails — every non-starter pilot is buy-to-fly, priced $0.50–$1.00, settled on Base. |
| Consumables | Health/shield/revive/XP-boost — bounded comeback aids, tuned so a spend is never a score multiplier. |
| Sponsored Cups | Time-boxed tournament boards with an admin-managed sponsor slot — a paid placement, not a paid advantage. |
| Referrals | Built-in referral system, ready to extend into a recruiter leaderboard. |

**Working rule:** the marketplace never affects a live run or score. This
is the credibility the leaderboard runs on, and it doesn't get traded for
revenue.

### Financing — token launch

Flagged as a real workstream, not a checkbox — a fundraising and legal
decision before it's an engineering one.

- **Purpose:** fundraising vs. utility/rewards token — different legal
  exposure, different builds.
- **Integrity rule:** any token utility follows the same rule as the
  cosmetics market — it never buys competitive advantage.
- **Status:** pre-legal-review. Next step is an internal memo settling
  purpose and regulatory posture before technical scoping starts.
